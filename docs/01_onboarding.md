# Brightdesk — New Hire Onboarding Guide

## Before Your First Day
- IT will email your account credentials 3 business days before your start date. If you haven't received them by then, contact it-support@brightdesk.example.
- Bring a government-issued ID for identity verification on day one.
- Your manager will send a calendar invite for a 30-minute welcome call on your first morning.

## First Week Checklist
1. Complete the HR intake form (personal details, emergency contact, tax forms).
2. Set up your company laptop and enroll in two-factor authentication.
3. Meet with your onboarding buddy (assigned by your manager) for a team walkthrough.
4. Review the Employee Handbook and acknowledge receipt in the HR portal.
5. Schedule 1:1s with your direct manager and skip-level manager within the first two weeks.

## Probation Period
All new hires have a 90-day probation period. Formal check-ins happen at day 30, day 60, and day 90. Full benefits eligibility (health insurance, 401k/pension matching) begins on day 1, but probation performance is reviewed before the role is confirmed as permanent.

## Equipment
Standard equipment (laptop, monitor, headset) is provided by IT. Requests for additional equipment go through the #it-requests channel and are typically fulfilled within 5 business days.
